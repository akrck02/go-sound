🗺️ Roadmap

What is left, in the order it is best done. Phase 1 (this repository: a context, players, WAV and Ogg Vorbis, suspend and resume) is done, and was heard on a phone in October 2026.

Phase 2: the sound of films

A film’s pictures play today (go-ui’s window.Video) and its sound does not. See the design for why it is split this way.

  1. go-renderer (android/video.c): open the film’s sound track with a second AMediaCodec (audio/mp4a-latm for AAC, audio/opus), from the same AMediaExtractor or a second one on the same file, and decode it a step at a time, as the pictures are. Hand out its samples (16-bit or float PCM, with the track’s rate and channels from the output format) without playing them. Rewind and the film’s end must reach the sound track too.
  2. go-ui (window/video.go): give the samples as a stream (Read(samples []float32), Format), in a small interface of go-ui’s own, so go-ui does not import go-sound. Let Video take a clock (func() time.Duration) and show the picture due by then, dropping or repeating pictures; with no clock it keeps its own, as today.
  3. Joining them: a small package (here as sound/film, or in go-ui-android) makes a go-sound player of the film’s stream and gives the film the player’s Position as its clock. Play, pause and rewind go to both.
  4. In a browser: the film’s <video> element plays its own sound in step with its pictures; unmute it after the person’s first touch (it is muted now so it can start without one).
  5. Try it: the gallery’s film (cmd/gallery/coast.mp4 has no sound track yet: make one with ffmpeg), on the emulator and a phone, checking the pictures keep up with the sound.

Phase 3: more

  • Audio focus on Android: ask for it when a sound starts (AudioManager.requestAudioFocus through JNI, in go-renderer), lower or pause the sound when another application takes it.
  • Volume by kind: music and effects turned up and down apart.
  • MP3 and Opus: go-mp3 is archived; look at goxiph, kazzmir/opus-go and darui3018823/opus, and check them against reference files before choosing.
  • A better resampler: linear interpolation is fine between 44.1 and 48 kHz; far apart rates (8 kHz speech) would sound cleaner through a short windowed filter.
  • Hear the desktop and the web: both are built but not heard yet (the containers here have no sound device). A go-ui example with a button, run on a desktop and in a browser.
