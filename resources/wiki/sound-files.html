🎼 Sound files

Format Package Read
WAV wav Integer samples of 8, 16, 24 and 32 bits, floating point ones of 32 and 64, any channels, any rate. Written here: a header and samples
Ogg Vorbis vorbis Any Vorbis file, decoded in Go by jfreymuth/oggvorbis

Which to choose. Ogg Vorbis for music and anything longer than a second: about a tenth of the size of a WAV, free of patents, decoded in Go everywhere. WAV for very short effects played often, where decoding costs more than the bytes.

Both rewind when their source can seek, so a player can loop them:

//go:embed waves.ogg
var waves []byte

stream, err := vorbis.Decode(bytes.NewReader(waves)) // a bytes.Reader seeks

Not read yet. MP3 (its Go decoder, go-mp3, is archived) and Opus (Go decoders exist, but are young). A film’s sound (AAC or Opus inside an MP4) will come from the system’s own decoders, as its pictures do: see the design.
